From a mechanic’s counter to a distribution house.

Pradeep Sales began in 1980 as a small retail shop for the mechanics of Prayagraj. Three generations later, it distributes OE and aftermarket three-wheeler parts across Uttar Pradesh.

0+Channel partnersand growing every year

Our story

Late Shri Suresh Chandra Gupta opened a small mechanic-based retail shop in Prayagraj in 1980, selling spare parts over the counter to the city’s workshops.

Through the 1990s the business began acquiring distribution of the top brands of the time. The next generation, Mr. Deepak Gupta, a mechanical engineer, and Mr. Pradeep Gupta, a finance expert, took it further through partnerships with leading OE suppliers and OEMs.

An ERP system arrived in 2005 to manage inventory, Power BI and other BI tools followed in 2023, and today the third generation, Mr. Ankur Gupta, an electrical engineer, is bringing AI into the business.

Genuine and OE parts

Systems that scale

Four decades on the road.

Scroll to ride along. Each stop is a milestone that shaped Pradeep Sales.

  1. 1980

    A small shop for mechanics

    Late Shri Suresh Chandra Gupta opens a mechanic-based retail shop in Prayagraj, selling spare parts over the counter to the city’s workshops.

  2. 1990s

    Bringing in the top brands

    The business starts acquiring the leading brands of the time, and the retail counter grows into a distribution house.

  3. Second generation

    Partnering with OE and OEM leaders

    Mr. Deepak Gupta, a mechanical engineer, and Mr. Pradeep Gupta, a finance expert, take the business forward through partnerships with top OE suppliers and OEMs.

  4. 2005

    ERP for inventory

    An ERP system brings thousands of SKUs into one place, so stock, orders and billing stay in step.

  5. 2023

    Business intelligence

    Power BI and other BI tools are introduced to analyse sales, brands and receivables in depth.

  6. Today

    Third generation, AI in the business

    Mr. Ankur Gupta, an electrical engineer, leads the business and is putting AI to work for faster decisions and steady growth.
